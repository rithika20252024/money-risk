The Problem

Reconciliation Cycles That Never Fully Close

Banks, payment aggregators, and NBFCs process millions of transactions across UPI, SWIFT, Card networks, and CBS.

⚠️ The Operational Nightmare

• 2-5% of volume breaks due to gateway fee deductions, network timeouts, and truncated narrative strings.

• Traps hundreds of crores in unreconciled Suspense Accounts.

• Ops teams spend 7-10 days on month-end close manually hunting discrepancies across spreadsheets.

🛑 Why Current AI Fails

• Write Risk: Direct database writes by AI agents risk hallucinating ledger adjustments and corrupting books.

• Regulatory & PII Violations: Passing raw customer bank accounts or names to LLMs violates RBI/DPDP regulations.

• Irreversible Commits: Once an adjusting journal entry is booked, undoing it without audit trail triggers compliance penalties.

Track Match: Month-end close consuming a full week & Reconciliation breaks
The Solution

ReconGuard Architecture: Exasol + Governed AI

Combining Exasol's lightning-fast in-memory columnar engine with Drydock's governed write path.

1. Exasol Columnar SQL

Processes 95%+ of transactional volume deterministically in milliseconds using vectorized equi-joins, fee-tolerance logic, and time-drift windows.

2. Zero-PII AI Agent

Residual complex breaks are sanitized with cryptographic tokens and routed to Gemini Flash to interpret mutated narratives and merchant memos.

3. Byte-for-Byte Undo

AI suggestions stage on isolated shadow branches. Human compliance officers review row-level diffs with 1-click commit or exact byte-level undo.
